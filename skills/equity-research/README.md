# Equity research

Skills for analyzing listed companies and their equity: valuation, financial-statement analysis, earnings quality and capital structure.

This domain is published as the `equity-research` plugin in the alpha-tango marketplace. Installing the plugin installs every skill listed below.

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [`equity-valuation`](equity-valuation/README.md) | Fair value (DCF, DDM, residual income), relative value against peers and history, DuPont and ROIC decomposition, and capital structure and cost of capital, all with sourced inputs and a standard report |

## Scope

In scope: single-company analysis of operating companies and financials whose equity trades publicly, and the peer and sector cross-sections that analysis needs.

Out of scope, and planned for other domains:

- portfolio construction, attribution and risk (`portfolio-management`);
- bond and curve analysis as an end in itself (`fixed-income`), although equity skills use the curve and a company's own debt as inputs;
- macroeconomic research (`macro-research`).

See [docs/roadmap.md](../../docs/roadmap.md) for the planned domains and skills.
